# Contributing

We welcome any contributions to the Swedish Reference Genome Portal.

The most common contribution is to submit a species to the portal. Full requirements and instructions are on the [Contribute page](https://genomes.scilifelab.se/contribute) of the website.

## In short:

- Download and fill in the [species submission form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ScilifelabDataCentre/genome-portal/main/species_submission/submission_form_templates/01-species-submission_v1.3.docx) and the [data tracks form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ScilifelabDataCentre/genome-portal/main/species_submission/submission_form_templates/02-data-tracks_v1.3.xlsx).
- Then either:
  - Submit a new species by Pull Request, as described in [species_submission/how_to_submit_through_pull_request.md](species_submission/how_to_submit_through_pull_request.md), or
  - Email the completed forms to srgp@scilifelab.se and we will set up the data for you.

Feel free to contact us at srgp@scilifelab.se at any point if you have questions.
