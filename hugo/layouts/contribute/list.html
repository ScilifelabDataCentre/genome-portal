How to add your data to the portal

Option A: Submit via Pull Request

1

Download the species submission form and the data tracks form and fill them in.

2

Fork the repository and follow our guide to generate your species pages.

3

Open a Pull Request for the Genome Portal team to review.

Option B: Submit via email

1

Download the species submission form and the data tracks form and fill them in.

2

Email the completed forms to srgp@scilifelab.se.

3

Our developers and data stewards will set up your data in the portal for you.

Questions at any stage? Email us any time at srgp@scilifelab.se.

Enjoy!
